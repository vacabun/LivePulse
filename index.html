🔒 Privacy Policy (100% Local Storage)

LivePulse is built with full privacy in mind. All schedules, festival lineups, itinerary selections, and user preferences remain 100% on your local device via browser localStorage. No personal data, schedules, or tracking cookies are ever transmitted to or stored on external remote servers.

⚠️ Legal Disclaimer & Notice

All timetables and event information managed within this application are provided solely for personal scheduling and planning convenience. LivePulse makes no warranties regarding the real-time accuracy of event schedules (including sudden organizer cancellations, delays, or stage shifts). Please always consult official event channels and organizer announcements for verified schedules.
